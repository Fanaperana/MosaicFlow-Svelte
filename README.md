## Who it's for

MosaicFlow is designed for people who work with complex, interconnected information: researchers tracking sources and hypotheses, students organizing course material and projects, writers mapping arguments and outlines, analysts connecting evidence and decisions, and teams capturing knowledge without locking it into a proprietary system.

It is especially useful if you prefer an infinite canvas for exploration but still want your notes, relationships, and pages stored as plain files that you can edit anywhere.
